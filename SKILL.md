## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design records the impression after looking, not everything that was seen. Keep the subject small, off-center, cropped or floating, with extreme white space like a page saved from an old travel manual. Use transparent watercolor, pale ink, hand line and a little paper texture. Stamps and collage scraps may appear only as sparse emotional clues. Reject a complete landscape display, heavy decoration and a photographic redraw.
